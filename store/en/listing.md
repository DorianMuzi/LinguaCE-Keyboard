## Full description (max 4000)
Write Chechen in the Latin alphabet or in Cyrillic, on a keyboard made for the language.

LinguaCE Keyboard is a project of the LinguaCE ecosystem, which supports learning and writing Chechen.

WRITE CHECHEN PROPERLY
• Latin Chechen alphabet: hold a letter for its variants (x → ẋ, s → ş, c → ċ, ç → ç̇, n → ŋ…).
• Cyrillic keyboard with the Ӏ (palochka) key.
• The КИР / LAT key switches between Latin and Cyrillic; long-press it to change keyboard.
• QWERTY or AZERTY for the Latin layout.
• Digits by long-press on the top row, like Gboard.
• The w stays available for foreign words.

SUGGESTIONS THAT LEARN
• Word suggestions from a base list and from the words you type, learned on your phone only.
• Type without accents: "noxciyn" suggests "noxçiyŋ".
• Personal dictionary: see, add and delete words, or erase everything.

COMFORT
• Light, dark or system theme; small, normal or large keys.
• Emoji panel with recents (long-press the comma).
• Toolbar: settings, emoji, undo and paste.
• Automatic capital letters and double space for a full stop.
• Optional key sound and vibration.
• Works with TalkBack.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No permission, not even Internet access: the keyboard cannot send anything.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Open source (Apache 2.0): https://github.com/DorianMuzi/LinguaCE-Keyboard

Want to help? In the app, you can choose to suggest words you use to enrich the suggestions. Nothing is sent without your action.
